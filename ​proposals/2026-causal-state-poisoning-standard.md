## Description
This pull request introduces a formal risk classification and mitigation standard addressing **Causal State Poisoning, Unbounded Multi-Agent Cascades, and Epistemic Drift** within large language model (LLM) and autonomous agent ecosystems. 

Synthesizing rigorous boundary evidence metrics, executable verification patterns from **IRONCLAD**, and biological-inspired continuity models from the **Living Mycelium Architecture (LMA)**, this proposal closes the structural gaps left by traditional perimeter defenses. It provides a standardized framework for systems operating under inevitable component, authority, and governance compromise.

---

## What This Covers (Scope & Unified Architecture)
1. **Multi-Agent Causal Lineage (IRONCLAD Integration):** Enforces cryptographic digest chaining (`prior_signal_digest` / action-log handoffs) across multi-skill and multi-agent execution paths, allowing verifiers to trace state integrity end-to-end without trusting single issuers in isolation.
2. **Epistemic Time-Trust & Execution Boundaries (BEAF Integration):** Establishes strict temporal and state validation models, ensuring execution windows, version selections, and authorization predicates are mathematically verifiable rather than self-declared assumptions. Separates execution topology (`evaluator_did === agentDid`) cleanly from policy provenance.
3. **Continuity-First Survivability (Living Mycelium Architecture Integration):** Mandates that systems prioritize truth-preservation and evidence retention over blind execution when anomalies, policy contradictions, or boundary risks occur (ensuring execution halts with verifiable evidence so continuity can be successfully rebuilt).
4. **Machine-Evaluable Counterexamples:** Replaces vague prose guidelines with deterministic, schema-validated test fixtures and invariants that prove runtime execution failures cleanly without human ambiguity.

---

## What This Solves (The Vulnerabilities)
* **Unbounded State Cascade (Poisoned Handoffs):** Eliminates the vulnerability where a compromised or drifting upstream agent passes a toxic payload that is blindly trusted downstream simply because individual session tokens check out.
* **Backdating & Policy Evasion:** Closes the loophole where actors attempt to exploit unanchored execution timestamps to masquerade under older, looser governance intervals or stale policy versions.
* **Blind Operational Drift:** Solves the danger of systems continuing to operate while internally corrupted by enforcing fail-closed boundaries and non-invalidation invariants that protect valid identity and continuity proofs during containment.

---

## Why It Matters (Strategic Impact)
* **From Naive Prevention to Resilient Survivability:** Shifts industry standards away from impossible 100% prevention walls, accepting compromise as a statistical certainty and focusing on tamper-evident recovery and truth-preservation.
* **From Heuristics to Cryptographic Proof:** Replaces subjective security checklists with machine-evaluable predicates, schema-driven test vectors, and rigorous boundary evidence analysis.
* **Unified Governance Pipeline:** Synthesizes cutting-edge research in distributed agent continuity, boundary evidence metrics, and executable verification suites into a cohesive, actionable standard for global security frameworks.
